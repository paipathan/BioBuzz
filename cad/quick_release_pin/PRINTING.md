# Quick release pin – print notes

Printed stand-in for the AndyMark am-2579 FTC field panel pin. Generated by
`make_pin.py`; `quick_release_pin.stl` is ready to slice (units: mm).

## Check after importing

The slicer should show **24.0 × 56.6 × 4.3 mm**. If it shows 0.024 × 0.057 or
610 × 1438, the slicer guessed the wrong units; rescale to mm.

## Orientation

Print it **flat, exactly as exported** (handle and shank both on the bed, the
engraved "ABHAY / WAS HERE" face up). The text reads upright when the pin hangs
handle-up in the field.
The prongs then bend along the layers. Never stand it upright: the prongs
would snap along the layer lines.

## Settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Material | PETG preferred (better fatigue life); PLA works |
| Supports | None (steepest overhang is under 0.4 mm above the bed) |
| Layer height | 0.12–0.16 mm |
| Walls / perimeters | 4–5 (makes the shank and prongs solid) |
| Infill | 100 % (or as high as you like; walls do most of the work) |
| Elephant-foot compensation | On, ~0.1–0.15 mm (keeps the 1.2 mm slot open on layer 1) |
| Plate | Print 4+ at once so each layer has time to cool |

## Checked dimensions (from the exported STL)

| Feature | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shank width | 4.60 mm | Link holes 0.190" (4.83), rail holes 0.201" (5.11) |
| Shank flats | 4.30 mm | Flat on the bed; limits wobble in the hole |
| Ridge width | 5.60 mm | Same as the steel ball (5.56) |
| Ridge lock point | 33.35 mm from handle face | 1.313" effective length |
| Head face to end of lead-in | 38.10 mm | 1.5" |
| Pilot tip | 3.40 mm wide, 1.5 mm long (39.6 mm total) | Lead-in; on bottom-rail joints the floor stops the tip with the ridge 0.8 mm clear of the link's bottom wall, so it never sits half-squeezed |
| Slot | 1.20 mm wide, 18.1 mm long | Prongs squeeze 0.39/side max, 0.6 available |
| Handle | 24 × 17 × 4.3 mm, 17 × 7 mm finger hole | 5 mm front/back bars carry the text |
| Engraving | Impact, 3.0 mm tall, 0.5 mm deep, top face only | Leaves 3.8 mm of handle under the letters |

The mesh is watertight, normals point outward, and the volume is 1710 mm³.

The engraving is on the top face, so it needs no supports or bridging; the
printer just leaves gaps in the last 3–4 layers. Leave ironing off, or the
letters may get smeared. The 0.12 mm layer height makes the letters sharpest.

## How it seats in the field (am-0481b)

- **Top rail:** the ridge pops through 16 mm before the handle reaches the rail, then
  slides freely until the handle sits on the rail.
- **Bottom rail:** the floor stops the tip before the handle reaches the rail. This is
  normal; the steel pins do the same. The ridge is then 7+ mm inside the channel, past
  the top wall and clear of the bottom wall.

## Fitting a test batch

Print 2 first and push them through a link, rail and link a few dozen times.

- Won't go in / too tight: lower `SHANK_D` to 4.55
- Rattles / falls out: raise `SHANK_D` to 4.65 or `BUMP_H` to 0.6
- Pull-out too stiff: lower `SLOT_START` from 20 to 18 (longer, softer prongs)

Then re-run `make_pin.py` in Blender to regenerate the STL.
